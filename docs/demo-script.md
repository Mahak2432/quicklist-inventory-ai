# QuickListInventoryAI: 3 to 4 minute demo script

Target length: about 3 minutes 40 seconds. Read each line at a steady pace. The times are where each scene should start.

## Before you record

- Sign in at https://quicklist-inventory-ai.onrender.com with the demo account.
- Open Shopify admin for Shyam Creations in a second window, on Products or Inventory.
- Have three sample photos ready: a grey hoodie, a block-print kurta and a running shoe. The app's `public/samples` folder has similar images.
- Empty the Snap page before you start, so the first photo is the one you planned.
- Note that making a mannequin photo takes about 30 seconds. Cut that wait out in editing, or keep it and talk over it.

## Scenes

### 1. Hook and sign-in (0:00 to 0:25)

On screen: the sign-in page, then the app's Snap page.

> Small sellers in India list the same product on three or four places, and they update the stock by hand every time something sells. QuickListInventoryAI fixes that. You take a photo, it writes the listing, and it keeps one stock count across your channels. Here I'm signed in to a demo shop.

### 2. Snap a photo and get the listing (0:25 to 1:00)

On screen: choose the grey hoodie photo. Show the listing filling in: title, colour, bullets, description, and the HSN suggestion.

> I'll take a photo of this hoodie. The app reads it and writes the title, the bullet points, the description and the search keywords. Every field is a suggestion, so I can edit anything. It asks only for what a photo can't show: sizes, stock and prices.

### 3. Photo tools: add, edit, white background (1:00 to 1:35)

On screen: tap Add photo, then Edit on a photo, crop to square, save. Then switch one photo to White background.

> A rough photo doesn't need a studio. I can add more photos, crop or rotate one, and brighten it. If I want a clean shop look, I switch a photo to a white background. Each photo chooses its own version.

### 4. Mannequin photo from a note (1:35 to 2:05)

On screen: start a new listing with the kurta photo and type "block print kurta, put on a mannequin". Show the result: the mannequin photo as the main image, with the original next to it.

> If I only have a flat photo, I can write what I want in plain words. Here I typed "put on a mannequin". The app makes a new photo for the listing, and the text still comes from my original photo.

### 5. Details in one line, then publish (2:05 to 2:35)

On screen: in the "Type the details in one line" box, type "Sizes S, M and L, 10 each, MRP 1299, sell 799", then Read it. Show the sizes and stock filled in. Click Publish to my stock.

> Now the details. I don't have to fill a form. I type one line in English, Hindi or Hinglish, and the app reads the sizes, the stock for each size, the MRP and my selling price. Then I publish.

### 6. The listing appears in Shopify (2:35 to 2:55)

On screen: the "Listed as ... with 3 SKUs" confirmation, then switch to Shopify admin showing the new product with its SKUs and stock.

> The same moment the product goes live in my stock, it is created in my Shopify store, with its photo, one variant for each size, and the stock. No CSV upload.

*Insert the Shopify screenshot here if you record without a live cut.*

### 7. Stock page and selling (2:55 to 3:15)

On screen: the Stock page with photos on each row. Tap minus one on a size, and point out the low-stock warning.

> The Stock page shows each SKU with its photo, so I can tell products apart without reading names. When something sells, I tap minus one. The other channels get the new count. Low stock is flagged in red.

### 8. Orders and WhatsApp (3:15 to 3:30)

On screen: the Chat page, with the message "sold 1 APP-GRE-03BE-M" and the reply.

> Sellers can also update stock from WhatsApp. A message like "sold one" or "stock" gets an answer straight away, and the sale updates every channel.

### 9. Close (3:30 to 3:40)

On screen: the Settings page showing Shopify connected, then the app's logo.

> QuickListInventoryAI runs on Neon Postgres, so the shop, products and stock stay safe across restarts. It is built for small Indian sellers who sell on more than one channel. Thank you for watching.

## Notes for editing

- Keep the photo and mannequin waits short. Speed them up or cut them.
- Show each feature once. The three things judges should remember are the listing from one photo, one stock count across channels, and the Shopify listing that appears on publish.
- If a scene fails during recording, re-record that scene only. Don't restart the whole demo.
